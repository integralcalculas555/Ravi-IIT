Q1 NCERT Standard Maximization

Ex 12.1 Type

Problem Statement

  • x + y ≤ 4
  • x ≥ 0, y ≥ 0

Mathematical Solution Steps

Step 2: Test origin (0,0): 0 + 0 ≤ 4 (True). The feasible region is bounded in the first quadrant.

Step 3: Evaluate $Z = 3x + 4y$ at corner points:

Q2 NCERT Standard Minimization

Ex 12.1 Core

Problem Statement

Minimize Z = -3x + 4y subject to constraints:

  • x + 2y ≤ 8
  • 3x + 2y ≤ 12
  • x ≥ 0, y ≥ 0

Mathematical Solution Steps

Step 1: Plot boundary lines: $L_1: x + 2y = 8 \implies (8,0), (0,4)$ and $L_2: 3x + 2y = 12 \implies (4,0), (0,6)$.

Step 2: Solve simultaneous equations for point of intersection $B$:
$(3x + 2y) - (x + 2y) = 12 - 8 \implies 2x = 4 \implies x = 2, y = 3 \implies B(2, 3)$.

Step 3: Corner Point Evaluation for $Z = -3x + 4y$:

O (0, 0)-3(0) + 4(0)0

B (2, 3)-3(2) + 4(3)6
C (0, 4)-3(0) + 4(4)16
Conclusion: Minimum value of Z is -12 occurring at point (4, 0).

Q3 NCERT Intersecting Maximization

NCERT Pattern

Problem Statement

Maximize Z = 5x + 3y subject to constraints:

  • 3x + 5y ≤ 15
  • 5x + 2y ≤ 10
  • x ≥ 0, y ≥ 0

Mathematical Solution Steps

Step 1: Intercepts for $3x + 5y = 15 \implies (5,0), (0,3)$ and $5x + 2y = 10 \implies (2,0), (0,5)$.

Step 2: Calculate Intersection Point $B$ by matrix/algebraic elimination:
Multiplying line 1 by 2: $6x + 10y = 30$; Line 2 by 5: $25x + 10y = 50$.
Subtracting: $19x = 20 \implies x = 20/19 \approx 1.05$. Substituting gives $y = 45/19 \approx 2.37$.

Step 3: Corner Point Table:

Corner Point Z = 5x + 3y Value
O (0, 0)5(0) + 3(0)0
A (2, 0)5(2) + 3(0)10
B (20/19, 45/19) 5(20/19) + 3(45/19) = 235/19 12.37 (MAX)
C (0, 3)5(0) + 3(3)9
Conclusion: Max Z is 235/19 (≈12.37) occurring at (20/19, 45/19).

Q5 Real-Life Manufacturing / Formulation Problem

Ex 12.2 Real-world

Problem Statement

A manufacturer produces two items A and B. Item A requires 1 hr of machine 1 and 3 hrs of machine 2. Item B requires 2 hrs of machine 1 and 1 hr of machine 2. Max hours available per day are 8 and 9 respectively. Profit on A is ₹250 and on B is ₹200.

Formulated LPP: Maximize Z = 250x + 200y subject to:

  • x + 2y ≤ 8
  • 3x + y ≤ 9
  • x ≥ 0, y ≥ 0

Mathematical Solution Steps

Step 1: Intercepts for $x + 2y = 8 \implies (8,0), (0,4)$ and $3x + y = 9 \implies (3,0), (0,9)$.

Step 2: Solve intersection $B$: $3(8-2y) + y = 9 \implies 24 - 5y = 9 \implies 5y = 15 \implies y = 3, x = 2$. Intersection is $B(2, 3)$.

Corner Point Z = 250x + 200y Value (₹)
O (0, 0)250(0) + 200(0)0
A (3, 0)250(3) + 200(0)750
B (2, 3) 250(2) + 200(3) ₹1100 (MAX)
C (0, 4)250(0) + 200(4)800
Conclusion: Max Profit is ₹1,100 by manufacturing 2 units of A and 3 units of B.

Q6 JEE Advanced Multi-Constraint Feasible Region & Area Analysis

Benchmark Problem

Problem Statement

Let feasible region R be bounded by inequalities:

  • 2x + y ≤ 8
  • x + 2y ≥ 4
  • x - y ≤ 2
  • x ≥ 0, y ≥ 0

Task: Determine the exact Maximum and Minimum values of Z = 5x + 3y over region R, and compute the exact Area of Region R.

Rigorous Mathematical Solution

Step 1: Vertices of Feasible Quadrilateral ABCD:

  • A (0, 2): Intersection of x = 0 and x + 2y = 4.
  • B (0, 8): Intersection of x = 0 and 2x + y = 8.
  • C (10/3, 4/3): Solving 2x + y = 8 and x - y = 2 simultaneously. Adding gives 3x = 10 $\implies x = 10/3, y = 4/3$.
  • D (8/3, 2/3): Solving x + 2y = 4 and x - y = 2 simultaneously. Subtracting gives 3y = 2 $\implies y = 2/3, x = 8/3$.
Corner Point Z = 5x + 3y Calculation Value
A (0, 2)5(0) + 3(2)6 (MIN)
B (0, 8)5(0) + 3(8)24 (MAX)
C (10/3, 4/3)5(10/3) + 3(4/3) = 62/320.67
D (8/3, 2/3)5(8/3) + 3(2/3) = 46/315.33

Step 2: Area of Quadrilateral Region R:

Splitting region along the vertical segment along the y-axis base $AB$ (length = $8 - 2 = 6$ units):
$\text{Area}(R) = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{Base} \times (x_C - x_D) = \frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times \left(\frac{10}{3} - \frac{8}{3}\right) = 3 \times \frac{2}{3} = \mathbf{5\text{ sq. units}}$.

JEE Final Result: Max Z = 24 at B(0,8) | Min Z = 6 at A(0,2) | Enclosed Area = 5 sq. units.
