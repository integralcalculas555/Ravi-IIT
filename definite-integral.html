Question 1

Evaluate

∫01(3x²+2x+1) dx

Solution

Given,

I = ∫01(3x²+2x+1) dx

Integrating term by term,

= [x³ + x² + x]₀¹

Putting upper limit (1):

= 1³ + 1² + 1 = 3

Putting lower limit (0):

= 0

Therefore,

∫₀¹(3x²+2x+1)dx = 3
